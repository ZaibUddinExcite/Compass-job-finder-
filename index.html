          Privacy by default

          Your job search is personal.

          This preview stores your profile, role drafts, and tracker in this browser only. Export a copy whenever you wish, or clear it with one button.

          It does not log in to job boards, submit applications, or send messages. Social posts are treated as leads until you verify an official route.

          It gives a reading of the posting, not immigration or employment advice. Where a requirement is unclear, it says so.

          Built to help people make fewer, better job-search decisions.
